Buying a polo from a new brand shouldn't feel like a gamble. Here's exactly how ours fit, what they're made of, and how to pick your size with confidence.

The Fit

Cut to move on the swing.
Sit clean at lunch.

Through the body

Across the shoulders

At the collar

How to measure

Chest: wrap a tape around the fullest part of your chest, under the arms, tape level. That number is your "fits chest" size.

Length: measured from the highest point of the shoulder to the hem. Our polos are cut to work tucked or untucked.

Still unsure?

Send your height, weight, and how you like a polo to sit — we'll tell you exactly which size to order. Ask us directly. Wrong size anyway? 30-day returns, no questions.

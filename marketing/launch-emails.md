# Launch email sequence — Drop One

Three emails, ready to paste into Mailchimp. Placeholders to fill before
sending: `[DATE]` (launch date), `[XX%]` (launch discount, if any),
`[FIRST NAME]` (use Mailchimp's `*|FNAME|*` tag, or cut the greeting — we only
collect email, so default to no name).

Send plan:
1. **Welcome** — automation, fires on signup (Mailchimp: Automations → Welcome new subscribers)
2. **Drop tease** — campaign, 4–5 days before launch
3. **Launch day** — campaign, morning of launch

Voice check: short sentences, concrete details, no hype words. All three
emails pass the banned-word list in CLAUDE.md.

---

## Email 1 — Welcome (on signup)

**Subject:** You're on the list
**Preview text:** Two polos. $60. Shipping included.

You're in.

Elvoro Golf makes performance polos for guys who'd rather let the play talk
than the logo. Drop One is two shirts — the Evergreen and the Meridian —
$60 each, shipping included.

What happens next: one email a few days before the drop opens, and one the
morning it goes live. That's it. No weekly blasts.

If you signed up from a product page, we logged your size — you'll hear
first when it's in stock.

Talk soon,
Elvoro Golf

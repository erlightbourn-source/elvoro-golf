Shipping

We ship within the United States via tracked carriers. Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Standard shipping is complimentary on every order — it's included in the price, with no minimum.

  • Standard: 3–5 business days
  • Expedited: 1–2 business days (calculated at checkout)
  • You'll receive tracking by email as soon as your order ships.

International shipping is coming soon — join the list to hear first. Duties and taxes, where applicable, are the responsibility of the recipient.

Returns & Exchanges

If a piece isn't right, return it within 30 days of delivery for a refund or exchange. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition with tags attached.

  • Start a return from your order confirmation email or by contacting us.
  • Exchanges for a different size ship free.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method once the return is received and inspected.
  • Final-sale items, if any, are marked at the time of purchase.

Privacy

We respect your privacy and collect only what we need to fulfill orders and improve your experience. This includes contact details, shipping information, and order history. Payment information is processed securely by our PCI-compliant payment providers — we never store full card numbers.

  • Email subscribers can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any message.
  • We use cookies for essential site function and analytics; you can manage these in your browser.

To request access to or deletion of your data, reach us through our contact page.

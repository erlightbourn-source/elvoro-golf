Privacy

Your data, plainly.

We collect as little as we can to run a shop and a mailing list, we tell you who touches it, and we never sell it. Here's exactly how it works. Last updated August 14, 2026.

What we collect How we use it Who we share it with Cookies Your choices Security Reaching us

What we collect

Two things, really. If you join the list, we collect the email address you give us (and, from a product notify form, the size and polo you're interested in). If you place an order once the shop is live, we collect the details needed to fill it — your name, shipping address, email, and what you bought.

Payment is different: when you check out, your card details go straight to our payment processor over an encrypted connection. Elvoro never sees or stores your full card number. We also log basic technical data your browser sends — things like your IP address — purely to keep the site running and to slow down spam and abuse.

How we use it

To do the two things you came for: send you the founding-list and launch emails you asked for, and fulfil, ship, and support your order. We also use your message if you write in through the contact form, and the technical data to protect the site. That's the whole list. We don't build advertising profiles, and we don't use any automated or AI system to make decisions about you.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information, full stop. We do rely on a few trusted services to operate, and share only what each one needs:

  • Mailchimp — stores our mailing list and sends our emails.
  • Our payment processor — a PCI-compliant hosted checkout provider handles your card and processes the payment at launch. Because it takes payment directly, it never passes your card number back to us.
  • Shipping carriers — receive your name and address to deliver your order.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website.
  • FormSubmit — relays contact and signup form messages to our inbox.

We'll only ever hand over data beyond this if the law requires it.

Cookies

This site keeps cookies to a minimum — the ones needed for it to work and, at most, plain analytics so we can see which pages people read. We do not run advertising or third-party ad-tracking cookies. When you check out, our payment provider may set its own cookies to complete the transaction securely; those are governed by that provider's policy. You can clear or block cookies in your browser at any time.

Your choices

You're in control of your data:

  • Unsubscribe from emails any time using the link at the bottom of any message — it takes one click.
  • Want to see what we hold, or have it deleted? Ask us through our contact page and we'll take care of it.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and card payments are handled entirely by a PCI-compliant processor, so sensitive payment details never sit on our side. We keep the personal data we do hold limited to what's genuinely useful and no longer than we need it.

Reaching us

Questions about your data, or a request to access or delete it? Reach us any time through our contact page and a real person will get back to you.

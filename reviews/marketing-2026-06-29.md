# Elvoro Golf — marketing & design review (2026-06-29)

Advisory only. No site files were changed by this review. Ranked by impact vs effort.
Site reviewed: https://erlightbourn-source.github.io/elvoro-golf/ (pre-launch, two polos, $60, waitlist only).

## Top priorities

### 1. Add a concrete early-access offer to the signup
- **Issue:** The join CTA promises access and first pick, but no tangible reward to act today. Access-only perks convert worse than a clear offer.
- **Recommendation:** Give list members a real launch-day offer (e.g. a set % off Drop One, or "first 100 get a free hat"). Put it in the button and hero.
- **Impact:** High · **Effort:** Low

### 2. Put a real person behind the brand
- **Issue:** The story reads as written by no one in particular — first-name-only founder, no face, no business name. New-brand visitors hesitate without it.
- **Recommendation:** Real founder name, a photo, a two-line story, and the registered business name in the footer.
- **Impact:** High · **Effort:** Medium

### 3. Show fabric proof
- **Issue:** Copy claims four-way stretch and a hand that holds up, but there's no fiber content, weight, or mill named. Buyers at this level want the spec.
- **Recommendation:** On Fit & Fabric and the PDPs, list fiber content, fabric weight (gsm), and care. A close macro of the weave helps.
- **Impact:** High · **Effort:** Medium (needs the sample)

## Worth doing soon

### 4. Set a real launch window
- **Issue:** "Launching soon" is honest but gives no reason to come back.
- **Recommendation:** A month or a date ("Drop One — March 2026") creates a return trigger and pairs well with the early-access offer.
- **Impact:** Medium · **Effort:** Low

### 5. One line on why Elvoro vs the incumbents
- **Issue:** "No loud logos, performance fabric" is a category several brands already occupy. Nothing yet says why this one.
- **Recommendation:** A single sharp differentiator — the price-to-make ratio, the two-shirt focus, a specific construction detail — stated plainly near the top.
- **Impact:** Medium · **Effort:** Low–Medium

### 6. Remove the internal note from the coming-soon footer
- **Issue:** The coming-soon page footer reads "Secure your handles · Trademark pending" — that's a to-do for the owner, not a message for visitors.
- **Recommendation:** Replace with a real tagline or live social links once handles exist.
- **Impact:** Medium · **Effort:** Low

### 7. Add social proof as it becomes real
- **Issue:** No reviews, follower counts, or press yet — expected pre-launch, but it's the missing trust layer.
- **Recommendation:** When the list grows, show a real signup count; after launch, surface first reviews and any UGC.
- **Impact:** Medium · **Effort:** Medium

## Polish

### 8. Lead with on-body imagery higher on the homepage
- **Issue:** The floating product cut-outs read premium, but younger buyers want to see the polo worn.
- **Recommendation:** Pull an on-body shot higher up, or let the card hover-to-on-body extend to a small lifestyle band.
- **Impact:** Low–Medium · **Effort:** Medium

### 9. Set shipping-timing expectations on the PDP
- **Issue:** Gift buyers can't tell when they'd receive an order.
- **Recommendation:** Once a date exists, add "ships within X days of launch" near the notify box.
- **Impact:** Low · **Effort:** Low
